Still open before a final release

  • Independent chapter and explained-answer review against the actual normative definitions.
  • Expanded API behavior, algorithms, conversions and exceptions at the promised depth. The separate declaration register does not replace these explanations.
  • Complete keyword/value/default registries and excluded media/drag event inventories.
  • Full ARIA restrictions and accessibility mapping reconciliation.
  • Cross-browser and relevant assistive-technology checks, plus a security review of the delivered experience.
  • A tagged, accessibility-reviewed PDF workflow. The supplied PDF has selectable text, links and bookmarks, but is not claimed to be a tagged accessible PDF.
  • Remaining source attribution and technical approval. Deployment is self-managed on Vercel; no repository integration is required.

Browser smoke tests exercise this site and selected examples. They do not establish universal browser support. Nu tests document conformance for its recorded version, not complete accessibility or security.
